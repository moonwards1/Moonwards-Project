Tip Mass Driver Calculator

A straight linear mass driver of the kind that could swivel at a skyhook tip. A reusable sled carries a drone cargo ship from rest to release speed, then brakes in a short section beyond the hub and is reused. The track is laid out so that the drive reaction is always carried in tension, by a carbon-nanotube spine anchored at the hub. Cordierite glass-ceramic backs the aluminium windings. Results update as you type; the i buttons explain each one.
