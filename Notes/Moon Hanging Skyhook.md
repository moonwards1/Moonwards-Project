# Moon Hanging Skyhook

Orbits the equator and interacts with:

- a lunar space elevator that runs from 

  - anchor points 20° north and south of the equator, 

  - through the Earth-moon L1 point, 

  - to a counterweight 222,000 km from the moon

- two mass drivers on the lunar equator

  - one that launches cargo on small shuttles to the skyhook receiving deck

  - one that catches these shuttles as they return, and also catches momentum exchange shuttles originating on the space elevator

## Basics

- Top station

  - altitude 2000 km above surface

  - angular speed: 2.9 km/s, 2.53 x the speed of a free circular orbit at that altitude

  - v_inf speed 2.4 km/s

  - outward pull felt: 1.9 m/s^2

- Centre of Mass (CoM)

  - altitude 275 km above surface

  - at orbital speed, which is 1.56 km/s at that altitude

- Foot altitude

  - altitude 10 km above surface

  - angular speed: 1.36 km/s, 0.81 x the speed of a free circular orbit at that altitude

  - impact speed of drop from foot: 1.37 km/s

  - inward pull felt: 0.55 m/s^2

## Tethers

- Made of silica fiber - silica purified from lunar regolith and drawn into fiber in orbit, under fine thermal control. 

  - Due to its purity and freedom from defects - tensile strength: 17 GPa

  - With sizing, protective sheaths, and sunshade materials - density: 2.3 g/cm^3

  - Safety factor: 3

  - Lower tether ratios - taper: 1.03 mass 0.056 x load carried

  - Upper tether ratios - taper: 2.13 mass 0.52 x load carried

## Facilities

- Top station 

  - 2000 km altitude

  - peak mass ~40 kT

  - Contains reboost engines and ship release facilities

- Release deck

  - 949 to 950 km altitude (covers this span)

  - peak mass ~10 kT

  - releases capsules headed to Earth with cargo

- L1 deck

  - 750 km altitude

  - for traffic to and from the space elevator, which arrives at either end with almost no relative speed

  - mostly used to receive ships that are then lowered to the foot and released to be caught on the surface

    - The momentum transfer via the Coriolis force that this provides means the station's own reboost engines are used minimally as backup and fine-tuning

  - peak mass ~50 kT

- Anchor station

  - 0.2 km from CoM, and large enough that the CoM is always within its volume

  - 25,000 kT - huge production facilities, docks, plus mass accrued to act as a momentum bank stabilizing the structure

- There is also ~60 kT of equipment distributed along the upper tether, and 50 kT along the lower tether

  - tracks for the climbers, solar panels, power lines, climbers moving between facilities, maintenance and repair robots

- Receiving deck

  - 233 km altitude

  - receives many small shuttles each orbit from the launcher mass driver 

    - altitude is set so that these shuttles can arrive at low relative speed, and shuttles can be launched over a fairly long window, do a very small circularizing burn, and then another small burn at periapsis that adjusts their orbits so they all arrive at the right altitude, at the right time to all be caught by the skyhook (please take my word for this rather than doing all the math)

  - peak mass ~8 kT

- Foot station

  - 10 km altitude 

  - mass ~500 kT

  - Very heavy because that puts the CoM very close to the anchor station, for little extra tether mass
